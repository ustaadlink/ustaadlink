About UstaadLink

A home tuition network built around a simple idea: connect learners with the right tutors.

Our Mission

Make quality home learning easier to access.

UstaadLink is designed to connect students and parents across Peshawar with tutors who match their academic needs, subjects, level, and preferred area.

Our goal is to make the process of finding home tuition simple, transparent, and convenient.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which areas do you cover?

UstaadLink is intended to serve home tuition requests across Peshawar. Coverage can depend on tutor availability.

Which students can use UstaadLink?

School, college, and university students can submit a tutor request according to their subject and level.

Can tutors register?

Yes. Tutors can use the Become a Tutor page to submit their qualifications, subjects, experience, and preferred areas.
